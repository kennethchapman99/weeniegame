# Generated Level Area Prop Pack

Couch-test transparent cartoon level plates for missions whose spaces should not read as the generic backyard.

- `kitchen_floor_area` and `kitchen_counter_wall` stage Kitchen Falling Food Frenzy as an indoor kitchen around the controller-owned counter and safe bowl. Blanket Catch reuses `kitchen_floor_area` directly (V3.4) since its briefing is the same kitchen counter. The approved `kitchen_floor_area_storybook_v02` paintover replaces the blockout grid with cream-and-sage tile, a compact bowl mat, and a mouth-scale tennis ball while preserving the open play lane. `kitchen_counter_wall_storybook_v02` replaces the counter's draft rectangles with honey-oak cabinet panels, brass knobs, a pale blue-gray worktop, and compact ingredient boards at the ends while keeping the middle landing lane clear. The indoor generator promotes both approved sources.
- `car_interior_cabin` and `car_balance_lane` (RETIRED 2026-07-14): replaced by the Car Ride Chaos backseat set in `ArenaFinal/Props/CarRide/` (see `Assets/Art/ReferenceOnly/GeneratedCarBackseat/`), generated by `tools/art/generate_car_backseat_pack.py`.
- `diningroom_floor_area` (V3.4, 2026-07-19) stages Table Stealth's dinner-table steak heist.
  Its approved `diningroom_floor_area_storybook_v02.png` paintover adds restrained sideboard/chair
  context plus a water bowl and knotted rope toy at the perimeter.
  `livingroom_floor_area` stages both Chaos Machine's Rube Goldberg den and Thunderstorm Comfort's
  storm-shelter huddle, which share one den fantasy per their briefing art. Its reviewed
  `livingroom_floor_area_storybook_v02.png` paintover (2026-07-23) replaces the earlier flat rug/
  plank draft with human-scale edge furniture, a quiet gameplay center, and dog-toy clutter in the
  established flat-storybook language. `tools/art/generate_indoor_level_area_packs.py` promotes that
  approved source when regenerating the pack and retains its deterministic geometry as a fallback.

All three V02 paintovers were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-07-23
using the corresponding blockout as a footprint reference. Prompts required the exact rounded
4:3 footprint, transparent outer corners, warm flat-cel storybook rendering, coherent upper-left
lighting, human-scale perimeter props, a quiet central gameplay lane, and no dogs, people, text,
letters, UI, or objective markers.

`MissionLevelAreaArt` temporarily suppresses the shared backyard's decorative root and the pool's
visual-only child while any indoor area is active. This prevents the outdoor pool, stepping stones,
and route guides from compositing over room art while leaving gameplay systems and colliders alive.

These are generated placeholder assets for readability review. Controller-owned markers, colliders, and deterministic mission state remain authoritative.
